Privacy Policy

Your privacy is not a feature — it's the foundation of everything we build.

Last updated: May 24, 2025

The Short Version

Wireless Mic collects zero user data. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no user accounts, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising IDs. Your audio data never leaves your local network and never passes through any third-party server.

1. Data Collection

Wireless Mic does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • Audio Data: Your microphone audio is streamed directly from your Android phone to your Windows PC over your local Wi-Fi network or USB connection. Audio data is encrypted with AES-256 encryption and is never sent to any external server, cloud service, or third party.
  • Usage Analytics: We do not use any analytics frameworks, usage tracking, or telemetry. We have no way to know how many people use the app, how often they use it, or what features they use.
  • Crash Reports: We do not collect crash reports. If the app crashes, no data is sent anywhere.
  • Device Information: We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or any other device information.
  • User Accounts: Wireless Mic does not require or support user accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or registration process.

2. Network Communication

Wireless Mic communicates exclusively over your local network:

  • Audio packets are sent via UDP from your Android device to your Windows PC on port 55555.
  • All audio packets are encrypted with AES-256 CBC before transmission.
  • No data is sent to any external server, API, or cloud service.
  • The app does not require internet permission and functions completely offline.

3. Permissions

The Android app requires the following permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required to capture audio from your phone's microphone. This is the core functionality of the app.
  • Network (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to send audio data over your local Wi-Fi network to the Windows client. Despite the INTERNET permission name, no data is sent to the internet.

4. Third-Party Services

Wireless Mic does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. The app is fully self-contained and operates independently. There are no ads, no ad networks, no social media integrations, and no external dependencies that could compromise your privacy.

5. Open Source Transparency

Wireless Mic is fully open-source under the MIT License. The complete source code is available at github.com/satnam6g/flumo_mic. You can verify every claim in this privacy policy by inspecting the code yourself.

6. Children's Privacy

Wireless Mic does not collect any data from any user of any age. The app is suitable for users of all ages.

7. This Website

This website (mic.infinityfreeapp.com) is a static HTML site. It does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking technologies. No personal information is collected when you visit this website.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Given that our policy is "collect nothing," we don't anticipate significant changes.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through GitHub Discussions or by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
